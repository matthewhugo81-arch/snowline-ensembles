Data sources, availability & chart guide

Read the ensemble

Each thin line is an individual ensemble member. The pink line is the arithmetic mean of the available members; the shaded band spans the 10th to 90th percentiles. It is an ensemble range, not a calibrated confidence interval. Missing values are gaps, never zeros.

Click or hover to inspect the same time in every panel. Drag horizontally to zoom all charts together. Use the time slider for keyboard inspection. Each panel also has its own optional minimum and maximum.

Snow signal

The snow panel shows the share of available members forecasting at least 0.1 cm of snowfall in the selected accumulation period. This is model agreement, not a calibrated probability or a prediction of settling snow.

Source & timing

Live ensemble data: Open-Meteo Ensemble API. The latest feed can be updated as new forecasts arrive. It does not provide a verified initialization time in this response, so retrieval time is shown rather than a guessed model run.

Hourly values may be interpolated from coarser model output. Temperatures are sampled at the displayed times. Precipitation and snowfall are summed over complete preceding 1-, 3- or 6-hour windows; incomplete windows remain missing. Forecast availability differs by model and variable.

MOGREPS here means the global 18-member system (MOGREPS-G). The feed currently supplies no 850 or 500 hPa member temperatures. No deterministic values are substituted.

The separate operational GFS line in the reference image is not included: this view uses ensemble members and their control only.
